Flow is not fixed pressure

Paddle position, puck resistance, and pressure interact

The paddle meters water flow. It does not save a fixed bar setting, and the same position can show different pressure as the puck erodes.

V3 low-flow automation makes a repeatable restricted-flow start or finish. Programmed pre-infusion separately turns the pump on, then off. Combining both is powerful but should come after a repeatable full-paddle shot.

Out-of-box tuning order
  1. Warm at least 20–25 minutes with the portafilter installed; the E61 group trails the boiler display.
  2. Use the 16–18g basket around 18g, preserve headspace, and stop every shot by scale weight.
  3. Turn pre-infusion and low-flow OFF. Put the paddle fully open and dial ratio, grind, and puck prep.
  4. Adjust brew temperature in 2°F (1°C) steps by taste only after shots repeat.
  5. Choose one experiment: low-flow start, low-flow final, programmed bloom, or manual paddle—not several at once.

Water: Follow Lelit’s limits: 0–110 ppm total hardness as CaCO₃, 40–75 ppm carbonate hardness, pH 6.5–7.5, and no chlorine.

V3 programmed flow controls

PI ON pumps water, PI OFF pauses the pump, low-flow START restricts flow until its timestamp, and low-flow FINAL restricts from its timestamp to lever-down. The paddle can further restrict every stage.

Version gate: V1/V2 have manual paddle control but not factory V3 low-flow automation. Confirm the LCC options or an authorized V3 conversion.

Pressure and paddle calibration

The small group gauge measures pressure near the puck; the chassis gauge reports pump-circuit pressure. A difference is normal. Dial flavor and flow, not a decorative 9-bar target.

With no portafilter, pressure should be near zero regardless of paddle. Never use an open group flow test to set pump pressure.

Advanced: The external rotary-pump screw changes the ceiling for every shot. Photograph the original setting, make no change until a repeatable standard recipe exists, and use qualified service if diagnosis requires opening panels or energized calibration.

Water supply and technical menu

Tank and plumbed operation share the V3 pump/solenoid logic. Do not assume a half-raised lever provides safe line-pressure pre-infusion; V3’s safety solenoid changes older E61 behavior.

Technician boundary: Record every factory PID/offset value before entering the technical menu. Annual hydraulic descaling, electrical work, internal leaks, and pressure calibration belong to qualified service.

What P1 and P2 really save

P1 dark, P2 light—with two global controls

The buttons remember their own total pre-infusion and timed auto-stop. They do not save a grind, beverage weight, brew temperature, or independent steam-vs-bloom mode.

The programmed stop time includes every pre-infusion second. A 40-second P2 with 12 seconds of pre-infusion leaves about 28 seconds for the full-pressure phase.

Out-of-box tuning order
  1. Warm for 20–30 minutes with the portafilter installed. Use a short purge, not a large cooling flush.
  2. Use the stock 14–18g double basket around 17–18g; confirm its engraving and preserve headspace.
  3. Turn timed auto-stop OFF while dialing. Stop by beverage weight on a scale.
  4. Lock dose and puck prep, choose a ratio, then adjust grind for controlled flow.
  5. Move brew temperature in 2°F steps by taste. Test pre-infusion only after the baseline repeats.
  6. A/B test steam, bloom, or no pre-infusion. There is no universal roast-to-mode winner.

Water: Use mineralized, espresso-safe water—not straight distilled/RO. Track hardness and alkalinity separately, and replace the resin filter by hardness and liters used.

Hidden LCC controls

With the machine off, hold + and − while switching on; keep holding until KPc appears. Photograph every original value first. Incorrect timing can reset the controller.

Amber risk: Firmware defaults vary. Change one subsystem at a time and give PID changes several days before judging them.

Pressure and OPV

The front gauge during a shot, a blind-basket static ceiling, and pressure at the puck under calibrated flow are different measurements. A factory reading around 10–10.5 bar static can be near 9 bar under flow.

Do not grind merely to force 9 bar, and do not open the machine only because a blind basket reads 10–10.5. First verify taste, repeatability, puck preparation, and flowing-shot behavior.

Red risk: Opening the chassis exposes mains voltage, hot boilers, and pressurized plumbing and may affect warranty. Confirm a V3, unplug, cool fully, mark the factory position, make only a tiny reversible change, and refit the cover before every test. Older PL92T instructions differ; technician service is the safe path.

Experimental modifications

The classic Elizabeth has programmable pre-infusion, not true live flow control. E61/Bianca paddle kits do not fit merely because the basket standard is called LELIT58.

Technician-only: Pump dimmers, triac control, sensors, Gaggiuino-style conversions, wiring changes, and pressure-profile retrofits lack mature Elizabeth-specific consensus. This app does not provide wiring steps.

Elizabeth cleaning guide

After every use: Wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth, release a little steam, and clean the filterholder.

Weekly: Unplug and cool the machine, then wipe it with a soft cloth and plain water. Do not use abrasive cleaners or immerse it.

Monthly: Backflush with the blind filter and 3–5 g of espresso-machine detergent. Run the LCC backflush cycle, rinse the group and filterholder, brush the group and gasket, then repeat the cycle without detergent.

Important: Never put descaling products in the water tank. Replace the resin filter according to the capacity printed on its packaging.

Lelit Elizabeth PL92T manual, pages 23–25
Bianca cleaning and service guide

After every shot / daily: Empty and wash basket and portafilter, brush the group gasket, wipe and purge the steam wand after milk, and hand-wash the drip tray with neutral soap.

Weekly group care: Soak metal portafilter parts and baskets for 15 minutes; keep the wooden handle dry. Detergent backflush 10 seconds on / 10 seconds off ×10, rinse, then repeat ×5 with water only.

Weekly wand care: Use the manual’s milk-system detergent cycle: 500 ml solution, steam 5 seconds / rest 5 seconds ×10, then repeat with 1 liter of fresh water.

Filter: Replace by 70 liters (about 28 full 2.5-liter tanks), at four months maximum, or after one month unused—whichever comes first.

Annual service: Lelit assigns hydraulic descaling, inspection, and lubrication to a qualified technician. Unplug and cool before owner cleaning; keep wood away from water and steam.

Lelit Bianca PL162T V3 Extended Guide, pages 24–27
